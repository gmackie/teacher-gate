### Why we are asking you

You are being asked to take part because you are a teacher of [GRADE BANDS / SUBJECTS]. Taking
part is voluntary.

### What the study is about

We are testing software called Teacher Gate. It drafts feedback on student writing using
artificial intelligence (AI). A teacher reviews every suggestion, and nothing reaches a student
unless the teacher releases it. We want to learn how long reviewing takes, what teachers change,
and how to make the software easier to use.

We are testing the software, not you.

### What you will do

- One session of about [60] minutes, [IN PERSON AT LOCATION / ONLINE].
- You will review AI-drafted feedback on about [7] short pieces of student writing, and one
  summary of a class's answers, while thinking aloud.
- We will time each piece and note what you accept, change or reject.
- You will answer two short questionnaires and a few interview questions.

All student writing in the session is **[invented for the study / de-identified under AGREEMENT]**.
No real student will see any feedback you release in the session.
